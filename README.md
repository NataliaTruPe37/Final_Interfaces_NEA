In Mexico, there is a lack of a having savings culture and financial literacy, which causes that even young people who are in a privileged economic position fall into debt or default on payments

FineLight is an app that lets you enter your monthly expenses and prioritize them, with the goal of providing people between the ages of 18 and 23 (people who are on their way to becoming professionals) an easy way to manage their money

The interface design is based directly on a prototype previously developed in Figma, which can be accessed via this link:

## Setup instructions

When the app launches, a start button appears; clicking it takes the user to a short questionnaire that asks about their salary and how often they receive it (weekly, biweekly, or monthly).

From there, the user will be asked about major monthly payments they need to make (an opportunity to set fixed amounts for things like weekly groceries, water, rent if they are students that live away from home or simply an adult).

These will be the only payments that appear in the simulator once it starts, and from there, the user can add more and sort them by priority (Essential, Flexible and Lifestyle). With this they can directly go and manage their payments per month and see the result. The app will throw warnings if the user tries to buy expenses with lower priority if he hasn’t bought everything of the essential part.
